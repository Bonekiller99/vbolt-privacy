V-Bolt Universe

Privacy policy, rules and support for V-Bolt Universe. Last updated: November 18, 2026.

Questions, reports or data requests: voidarianbusiness@gmail.com

What is stored

To play you make a V-Bolt account. These are kept on our server:

  • Your username, the name you show to others, and a salted hash of your password. We never store the password itself.
  • Your avatar, your Voidarian Gems and stars, the items you own, your level, your achievements and your daily reward streak.
  • Your friends list, friend requests, and the players you blocked.
  • Which games you played and for how long, the games you liked or marked as favorites, and scores you put on leaderboards.
  • What games save for you (your progress), so it is there on every device you sign in on.
  • Reports you send about a player.
  • Your IP address with the time of sign-ups and failed sign-ins, used to stop abuse.

We do not ask for your real name, e-mail address, phone number, age, location or contacts, and the app does not use the camera or the microphone.

What other players can see

Your username, the name you show, your avatar, your level and stars, your short profile text, and whether you are online and what you are playing. A player you have blocked cannot see your profile.

The games

Every game in V-Bolt Universe is made by V-Bolt Studios, with our own tool (V-Bolt Engine) and our own programming language (Voidarian). Players cannot upload games. A game is given your display name and your avatar's look. A game cannot see your password, cannot spend your gems, and cannot change your account. A game can keep saved values and scores for you. A game never asks for your password or for personal details.

What we do not do

There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing is bought with real money. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone, apart from Cloudflare, which hosts the server.

Deleting your data

Open Settings and choose Delete my account. This removes your account, avatar, gems, items, friends, scores and saved progress, straight away. Deleting the app removes what is saved on your device.

Rules

By making an account you agree not to post hate, harassment, threats, sexual content, other objectionable content, or anyone's private information, and not to trick other players into giving away their password. There is no tolerance for this content or for abusive players.

You can block or report a player from their profile. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. Objectionable content is removed, and players who post it lose their accounts.

Children

V-Bolt Universe is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has made an account, e-mail us and we will delete it.
